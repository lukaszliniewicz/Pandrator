## #641: audio compatibility

Stale-only generation previously reused completed takes solely on block state,
without checking their effective voice or synthesis settings. It now compares a
versioned audio identity shared by direct speech-plan generation and ordinary
workflow execution/resumption. Identity covers the effective service/provider,
model, voice, language, material synthesis settings, selected conversion settings,
and managed/inline voice-reference content and reviewed transcript.

The audio.cpp identity resolver selects the same linked reference as synthesis:
the newest readable, ready sample. Editing an older unused sample does not
invalidate it. Reference changes after queueing or during execution are rejected
before synthesizing an affected block, rather than mixing reference versions.
Output/assembly choices do not unnecessarily invalidate speech takes.

Speech-plan topology and upstream text artifacts remain separate. Unchanged
blocks with compatible audio retain reuse after topology edits. UI and MCP expose
reuse reasons and counts; the UI explains that the plan is preserved while audio
needs regeneration. Existing takes remain available for manual playback/selection.
Legacy takes without identity evidence are conservatively regenerated by automatic
reuse; the patch does not retroactively guess their voice or delete them.
