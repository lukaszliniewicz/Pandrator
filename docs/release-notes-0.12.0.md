**Pandrator 0.12.0 adds finer speech editing and a reusable sound and music library.**

- **Shape the performance.** Edit speech, assign character voices, and add delivery directions in one generation workspace.
- **Change voices without starting over.** Regenerate the affected recordings, compare Before and After audio versions, and undo a recast when needed.
- **Add sounds and music.** Generate or import clips, save alternate versions, and mix effects or background music with narration and voiceovers.
- **Choose your controls.** Simple mode keeps everyday work focused; Advanced mode offers detailed speech editing and mixing.
- **Make a video book.** Export saved narration with timed reading text, adjustable colours, fonts, and layout.

This release also improves interrupted-work recovery and checks that generation uses the selected speech plan. Existing projects and audio history are retained.

Includes **Pandrator 0.12.0**, **Manager 0.9.29**, and **MCP 0.6.2**.
