# Reviewing and previewing work

Large review windows keep their content in the center and put secondary controls in a collapsible tools pane. Use **Tools**, **Find & compare**, **Details & compare**, or the named pane button to open it. On a phone, the pane opens over the workspace. Escape closes that pane first; another Escape requests closing the review. Maximize expands the workspace without reloading its contents.

## Captions and spoken text

In **Compare and refine**, use Tools to choose the editable revision, add comparison revisions, filter cues and search or replace text. The heading identifies what you are editing and whether it is the project's selected captions. Choosing an editable revision does not change the project's output selection.

**Display cues** edit subtitle presentation. **Spoken passages** edit spoken text, speaker and passage timing. Saving creates a new revision. The review retains your query, page and comparison context when tools collapse. Recheck audio opens the selected cue's evidence tools beside the table; generating evidence and accepting its text are separate actions.

Audio and video keep their playback state when tools collapse. The compact source transport remains available beside the editor. Open the media controls for the video picture, seeking and other playback options.

Document narration optimization saves a new reviewed artifact. Generation's **Review speech delivery** edits one segment's spoken delivery; saving can make existing takes stale. Regenerating after that save is an explicit option. Speech annotations and selecting or marking a whole speech plan reviewed remain separate operations.

## Read-only file previews

Artifact preview does not edit or select project output. Related files load only when you choose **Load comparison**. **Current file** and **Compared file** identify the two sides without assuming their chronological order.

Text downloads for preview are limited to 2 MiB. Long text is displayed in portions so large files remain usable on smaller screens. A warning identifies a clipped preview; **Download** retrieves the complete managed file. Large comparisons use paged side-by-side viewing instead of a full inline diff.

Speech-plan history previews an earlier version without activating it. Use its explicit restore or selection action when you want to change the active plan.

## Specialized editors

PDF editing keeps the canvas separate from **Pages & tools**. Applying edits queues a derived PDF and preserves the original. Closing with unapplied edits offers applying them, discarding them or continuing to edit.

Recording editing has a **Transcript & details** pane beside the recording and cut timeline. Saving a cut plan and rendering new media remain separate actions. Sound and voice auditions likewise separate their creation settings from the results you listen to.

Editable reviews protect unsaved changes when closing or navigating away. Pending saves remain visible; failures retain the draft for correction or retry. The server checks project permissions for every save or processing action.
