**Pandrator 0.14.0 adds team workspaces, optional project sharing, and more control over speech and review.**

- **Work together.** Enable individual accounts and shared workspaces for a team. Solo remains the default; existing projects stay private.
- **Share just one project.** Optional expiring links support project collaboration or external translation review. Submitted corrections need an editor's approval.
- **Review with more room.** Collapsible tools, phone drawers and better draft protection improve text, subtitle, delivery and PDF editing.
- **Direct the narration.** A dedicated annotation workspace brings speaker assignments and phrase directions together, with clearer sentence-packing choices and generation estimates.
- **More local speech options.** audio.cpp 0.9.3 adds Phonon, Ming 3.0 and XTTS routes, expands Kitten's languages and voices, and supports Drama 3 directions and optional disk-backed experts. XTTS's model licence is non-commercial; model and hardware requirements vary.
- **More reliable long jobs.** Exports can wait for disk space and resume. An optional second worker lets independent jobs overlap. Subtitle and video exports, update recovery and dependency compatibility also receive fixes.
